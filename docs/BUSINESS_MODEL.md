# Business Model & Features

## Core Product Offerings

### 1. Encrypted Health Records
- Secure, patient-controlled medical data storage
- Provider collaboration tools
- Emergency access protocols

### 2. AI-Powered Healthcare
- **Symptom Triage**: Intelligent specialist recommendations
- **Personalized Insights**: Health analysis using patient history
- **Preventive Care**: Risk assessment and early detection

### 3. Integrated Pharmacy
- **Prescription Fulfillment**: Seamless medication ordering
- **Delivery Coordination**: Logistics management
- **Inventory Integration**: Real-time stock availability

### 4. Web3 Financial Infrastructure
- **USDC Payments**: Stable, cross-border transactions
- **Embedded Wallets**: Frictionless user onboarding
- **Revenue Sharing**: Provider payment automation

## Revenue Streams
1. **Subscription Fees**: Platform access for healthcare providers
2. **Transaction Fees**: Small percentage on USDC payments
3. **Pharmacy Commission**: Percentage on medication orders
4. **Enterprise Licensing**: White-label solutions for hospitals

## Target Markets
- **Patients**: Individuals seeking secure health data management
- **Providers**: Doctors, clinics, and hospitals
- **Pharmacies**: Medication fulfillment partners
- **Researchers**: Anonymized data for medical research (opt-in)
